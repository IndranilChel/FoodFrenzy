WHO WE ARE

About FoodFrenzy

A smarter way to order food

FoodFrenzy is an online food ordering platform developed to provide customers with a simple, convenient, and user-friendly way to explore available food products and place orders online.

The application provides an organized environment where customers can browse food items, manage their cart, and complete their orders, while administrators can manage products, users, and order information.
